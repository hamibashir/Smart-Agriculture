**Component****Specifcation / Purpose**
**ESP32 Microcontroller**Dual-core 32-bit microcontroller with integrated Wi-Fi (802.11
b/g/n) and Bluetooth. Controls sensor data acquisition, local
threshold logic, irrigation actuation, and backend communication
via HTTP/REST protocol [8].
**Soil Moisture Sensor**Capacitive sensor (YL-69) that measures volumetric water content
in soil. Provides analog output (0–2560) proportional to moisture
level, enabling precise irrigation decision-making.
**DHT22 Sensor**Digital temperature and humidity sensor with calibrated output.
Measures ambient temperature (–40°C to +80°C) and relative
humidity (0–100%) for crop environment monitoring and weather
correlation [3].
**LDR Light Sensor**Light Dependent Resistor that measures ambient light intensity
(0–1023 lux equivalent). Supports day/night detection, optimal
irrigation timing, and crop photosynthesis analysis.
**Rain Sensor**Digital rain detection module (FC-37) with adjustable sensitivity.
Detects rainfall conditions to prevent irrigation during rain events
and conserve water resources [4].
**Relay Module**Single-channel 5V relay with optocoupler isolation. Switches
high-power pump/valve circuits (up to 10A at 250V AC or 30V DC)
based on ESP32 digital output or manual mobile app commands.
**Water Pump**12V DC submersible pump for irrigation actuation. Flow rate:
500–2000 L/hour depending on feld size and crop requirements [6].
**Power Supply**regulator). Separate 12V supply for pump with protection circuitry
(fuse, surge protection).
