Evaluation Questions:
•	Is the developed solution functional, and the documented requirements and design are reflected in it?
•	Are the test cases of the developed solution comprehensive and written formally?
•	Is the project published on the required platform or submitted as an installable package? 
•	By carrying out their assigned tasks, have the project team members shown their contribution toward completing their project?

Final II Evaluation (Internal) Rubrics
Question	Poor (0-4)	Average (5-6)	Good (7-8)	Excellent (9-10)
System Implementation	Most of the requirements are not implemented or incorrectly implemented	Most of the requirements are correctly implemented but there are serious inconsistencies with the design	Most of the requirements are correctly implemented and implementation is largely consistent with design	All requirements are correctly implemented according to the design
System Testing	Testing methodology is not defined, and there are no or very few test cases	Testing methodology is defined but test cases do not cover core functionality of the system	Testing methodology and test cases are clearly defined and test cases cover most of the requirements, but there are minor errors and omissions 	Testing methodology and test cases are clearly defined and test cases cover all requirements 
System Deployment	System is not deployed	System is deployed but not working or could not be demonstrated, and there are no details of deployment in project report	System is deployed and demonstrated, but details of deployment in project report are missing or incomplete	System is deployed and is available for live demonstration, and deployment chapter is well-written in project report
Team Work in System Development	There was no coordination among team members, and responsibilities of team members were not defined	Team members responsibilities were defined but most of the work was done by only one or two members	All team members made contribution in project activities, but distribution of work in team members was not even	All team members worked in close coordination, and each member made significant contribution in the project
